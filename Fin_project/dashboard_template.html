Market Power Index (MPI) Formula

$$\text{MPI} = (0.35 \times S_{\text{norm}} + 0.25 \times H_{\text{norm}} + 0.20 \times D_{\text{norm}} + 0.20 \times C_{\text{score}}) \times 100$$

  • Commercial Scale ($S_{\text{norm}}$, 35%): Log-transformed global sales.
  • Hit Rate ($H_{\text{norm}}$, 25%): Share of games exceeding genre median sales.
  • Genre Diversity ($D_{\text{norm}}$, 20%): Unique genres published (max 12).
  • Regional Consistency ($C_{\text{score}}$, 20%): Lower std dev of regional proportions = higher consistency.
