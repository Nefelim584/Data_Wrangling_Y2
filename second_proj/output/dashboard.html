Deliverable 4 Application

When intermediate scan checkpoints are missing or unrecorded, direct empirical journey reconstruction is impossible. A Random Forest Regressor (30 estimators, max depth 7) ensures 100% accounting completeness by predicting parcel emissions from shipment attributes.

Predictive Accuracy

Cross-validation yields R² = 0.825 and MAE = 2.07 kgCO2e across 1,370 verified complete journeys. Feature importance is dominated by payload ton-kilometers and air freight routing potential.
